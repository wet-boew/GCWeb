Alert with expand/collapse

Use an alert with expand/collapse when you need to show several alerts on the same page. Each alert only shows its heading until the user expands it, so stacked alerts take up less space.

Beware of false information online

For accurate information, always check official Government of Canada and provincial or territorial websites.

Check the latest scams alerts related to benefits and taxes.

Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit

The Government of Canada will increase the Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit (formerly the GST/HST credit) by 25% for five years starting in July 2026.

Learn more: Canada Groceries and Essentials Benefit
